title: "Why ATP² exists"
date: 2026-01-05
tags: ["governance", "infrastructure"]
summary: "A short note on what this project is for, and what it isn't trying to be."
---

Most tools for organizing get built by companies that need you to keep coming back, need your data to be worth something, or need a hierarchy somewhere in the stack — an admin, a platform owner, a terms-of-service you didn't write and can't change.

ATP² starts from a different assumption: the more distributed decision-making is, the more resilient the thing you're building becomes. That's true of mesh networks, and it turns out to be just as true of governance structures, water systems, and how a group decides what to do next.

This site is going to collect two kinds of things:

- **A knowledgebase** of how-to guides — mesh networking, low-tech water purification, tool-sharing systems, and whatever else turns out to be useful — written so someone with no prior background can actually follow them.
- **Writing** — shorter, more opinionated pieces about governance models, group facilitation, and the tradeoffs between different approaches, as we actually try them rather than just read about them.

None of this is finished, and it isn't supposed to be. Take what's useful, argue with what isn't, and if you build something better, we'd genuinely rather link to yours than keep maintaining ours.
